# Does Eating Healthy Actually Cost More?

A Python pandas analysis of U.S. grocery prices to test the belief that healthy food is expensive and show shoppers how to eat better without spending more.

## Key findings
- Within the same aisle, healthier products usually cost less per 100 g.
- "Healthy is expensive" shows up when comparing package prices, not price per 100 g.
- Sale tags appear more often on snacks, but the discount is no bigger.
- Store brands are cheaper and often healthier than national brands.
- The same product can cost noticeably more at a different store.

## Data
- Open Food Facts (product details, Nutri-Score, NOVA processing level)
- Open Prices (shelf prices matched by barcode)

The data files are too large for GitHub. They can be downloaded from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data) and Open Prices (https://prices.openfoodfacts.org).
